## Before you post

1. In Play Console, create a promotion for `pro_lifetime` with **one-time codes**, one per winner
   plus a few spare, and download the CSV. Set the end date at least two months after the giveaway
   ends, so winners have time to redeem.
2. Fill in the rules below (dates and number of winners) and put them in the caption, or at least the
   short version plus "Full rules in the pinned comment".
3. Pick winners by **best answer**, not at random. Judging on merit keeps it a contest of skill rather
   than a lucky draw, and the answers make good content. If you'd rather draw at random, say so in
   the rules and use a comment-picker tool.
4. On **Facebook**, people can only be asked to comment on or like the Page post. Facebook doesn't
   allow "share to enter" or "tag friends to enter", so the captions here only ask for a comment and
   a follow (a follow is fine on Instagram).

This is general guidance, not legal advice.

## Posting tips

- Post 2–3 times a week. Rotate feed posts with the story images, and put the matching story up on the same day as its feed post.
- A good order: carousel → post 1 → post 3 → post 4 → post 2. The carousel explains the app, so it is a good pinned post.
- Instagram and Facebook are both under Meta, so with Meta Business Suite one upload can post to both.
- Reels get the most reach on both apps. A 10–15 second screen recording of a real payment being recorded, with the post 1 caption, is a good next step. Record it on a test account or blur the amounts.
