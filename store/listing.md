## Full description

```text
Kahan Gaya Paisa? Every month, the same question. This app answers it — automatically.

Kahan Gaya Paisa records your spending the moment it happens, from your UPI app notifications and your bank's SMS alerts. No typing every payment in, no spreadsheets, no linking your bank account. And everything stays on your phone.

AUTOMATIC TRACKING
• Records payments from popular UPI apps such as Google Pay, PhonePe, Paytm and BHIM
• Reads bank SMS alerts from 40+ Indian banks and card issuers
• Understands UPI, debit and credit card spends, ATM withdrawals, NEFT, IMPS, RTGS, auto-debits, salary, interest, refunds and bank charges
• Picks up the amount, payee, bank and the last 4 digits of your account or card
• Ignores OTPs, promotions, failed and pending payments, and reminders
• Skips credit card bill payments, so your card spends are never counted twice
• A quick, silent alert confirms each payment as it is recorded

TODAY AT A GLANCE
• See what you have spent today the moment you open the app
• Set a daily budget and watch the day stay on track
• A short note each day on how your month is going

SMART CATEGORIES
• Sorts payments into categories like Food & Drink, Groceries, Fuel, Shopping, Bills and Travel
• Learns from you: fix a category or rename a merchant once, and future payments follow
• Needs Review shows the payments it wasn't sure about
• Give any category your own colour and icon
• Add notes and tags, and see where each tag's money went
• See every merchant you have paid, most-paid first

BUDGETS AND INSIGHTS
• Set daily, monthly or yearly budgets, overall or per category
• Get a heads-up at 80% and when you go over
• See where your money went, category by category, and tap any one for every payment behind it
• Momentum chart shows your running total, where the month is heading, and how it compares with last month
• Browse by month, year or all time

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
• No account, no server: your transactions never leave your phone unless you save a backup or export
• No ads, no analytics, no tracking
• Does not ask for SMS permission and never opens your inbox; bank alerts are read from the notification when they arrive
• Personal messages are checked on your phone and discarded, never stored
• Delete everything any time, or uninstall to remove it all

YOUR DATA, YOUR COPY
• Back up to a folder you choose, such as Google Drive
• Restore on a new phone in a few taps
• Export everything to CSV for your own spreadsheets
• Backing up, restoring and exporting are always free

ALSO
• Add cash payments by hand with one tap
• Optionally match payees to your contacts, so transfers show a real name
• Works on Android 8.0 and newer

PRO
Everything above is free. Pro, a one-time unlock or a yearly plan, adds room to organise and deeper analysis:
• Unlimited categories of your own (two are free)
• Ten more built-in categories, including House, Vehicle, Taxes, Family and Insurance
• More than five tags of your own, all charted
• Recurring payments: subscriptions and bills spotted for you, plus your own entries for rent and more
• Automatic backups, daily, weekly, fortnightly or monthly
• Compare this period with each of the six before it

HOW IT WORKS
After install, grant notification access when the app asks. That is how it sees payment alerts; it explains exactly what it reads before you turn it on. You can switch it off any time in Android Settings.

Kahan Gaya Paisa is an independent app by KHAL Tech and is not affiliated with any bank or payment app.
```
