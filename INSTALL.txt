KAHAN GAYA PAISA - TEST BUILD INSTALL
Android 8.0 or newer. Takes about 3 minutes.

Android blocks this app the first time. That is expected: the app reads
payment notifications, and Play Protect blocks any app installed outside
the Play Store that asks for that. There is no "install anyway" button,
so you switch the scan off for a minute and back on.


1. TURN THE SCAN OFF
   Play Store > your profile picture > Play Protect > gear icon >
   "Scan apps with Play Protect" > OFF > confirm

2. INSTALL
   Open the .apk file > Install
   If it says the app can't install apps, allow it on the screen it
   offers, then go back and tap Install again.
   Already have an older Expense Assistant or Kahan Gaya Paisa? Uninstall it first.

3. TURN THE SCAN BACK ON   <-- please don't skip this
   Same path as step 1 > ON

4. UNLOCK RESTRICTED SETTINGS   (Android 13 and newer)
   Settings > Apps > Kahan Gaya Paisa > 3-dot menu, top right >
   "Allow restricted settings"
   Not in the menu? Your Android doesn't need it. Go to step 5.

5. GIVE IT NOTIFICATION ACCESS
   Open the app > "Finish setup" card > Enable > Continue >
   switch on Kahan Gaya Paisa in the list Android shows

Skip 4 or 5 and the app records nothing and looks broken.


CHECK IT WORKS
Pay for something small over UPI. It should appear on the home screen
within a second or two.

WHAT TO TRY
- Are amount, payee and category right?
- Long-press a transaction to fix its category. It remembers that
  merchant next time.
- Add a cash payment with the + button.
- Set a budget, then watch the Budget tab.
- Profile > Your data: CSV export, backup, automatic backups.
- The ? button, top right, replays the tutorial.

GOOD TO KNOW ABOUT CAPTURE
The app captures from notifications only: payment app alerts and your
bank's SMS. A payment app that finishes without posting a notification
is still caught by the bank's SMS; if neither arrives, add it with +.

GOOD TO KNOW
- The app has no internet permission. Your transactions, contacts and
  notifications cannot leave the phone.
- No ads, no analytics. Uninstalling deletes everything it stored.
- Your build number is in Profile. Quote it when reporting a bug.

TROUBLE
- "App blocked to protect your device", only an OK button:
  step 1 didn't take. Check the toggle is really off, install again.
- "App not installed": an older build is still there. Uninstall it.
  That erases whatever it had recorded.
- Notification toggle greyed out: that's step 4.
- Installed but recording nothing: that's step 5. Profile > Capture
  should say "Enabled".

Anything else: send a screenshot, your phone model and Android version.
