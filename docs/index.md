# Kahan Gaya Paisa

An Android app by **KHAL Tech** that records your spending automatically from payment app
notifications and bank SMS alerts. Everything stays on your phone.

- [Privacy Policy](privacy-policy/)

## What it does

- **Records payments for you.** When Google Pay, PhonePe, Paytm, BHIM, your banking app or your
  messaging app posts a notification for a completed payment, the app records it within a second or
  two. About 45 banks and card issuers are recognised. Anything a notification never mentions, such
  as cash, can be added by hand.
- **Records each payment once.** A payment that arrives both from a payment app and as a bank SMS is
  kept once, while two payments of the same amount from different accounts are kept apart.
- **Sorts your spending.** Payments are filed into categories automatically, and every correction
  you make teaches the app how to file that merchant's next payment and what to call it.
- **Shows where the money went.** A today card against your daily budget, spending by category,
  how this week, month or year compares with the ones before it, tags, and a list of everyone you
  have paid with every payment made to each.
- **Keeps you on budget.** A daily budget, plus monthly and yearly budgets overall or per category,
  with an alert as you near or cross one.
- **Keeps your records safe.** Back up to a folder you choose, such as Google Drive, restore with a
  tap, or export everything as a spreadsheet.

## Free and Pro

Everything you record and everything you can read about it is free: capture, automatic
categorising, insights, budgets, tags, two categories of your own, backups by hand, and CSV
export. Pro is an optional one-time unlock or yearly plan that adds:

- Unlimited categories of your own, and ten more built-in ones, such as House Expense and Vehicle
  Expense, with house and vehicle payments filed automatically
- More than five tags of your own
- Recurring payments, found automatically or added yourself
- Automatic backups on a schedule
- Comparing your spending with each of the last six weeks, months or years, not just the last one

## Your privacy

- **Nothing leaves your phone** unless you save a backup or export yourself. There is no account,
  no server, no ads, no analytics and no crash reporting.
- **Only payment notifications are read**, from a fixed list of payment, banking and messaging
  apps. Everything else, including your personal messages and OTPs, is ignored and never stored.
- **No SMS permission, no screen reading.** Bank SMS are read only from the notification your
  messaging app shows.
- **Access is always your choice.** Notification access and the optional contact-name matching are
  explained in the app before you grant them, and can be turned off at any time.

The [Privacy Policy](privacy-policy/) covers all of this in full.
